## Project overview

This repository documents the design, observation, analysis, and research findings of CivicLens across the AgentVersa multi-agent legal simulation. It is maintained as a student research portfolio: the initial design is recorded before observation, predictions are preserved, scenario evidence is logged separately from interpretation, and later findings are compared against the original design.

### What is AgentVersa?

AgentVersa is a multi-agent legal simulation in which role-based AI agents interact under scenario-specific constraints and pressures. Agents may represent different institutional, civic, legal, or public-interest perspectives. The simulation is used here as an educational environment for studying agent behavior, cooperation, disagreement, uncertainty handling, escalation, and the effects of relationships and platform design.

### Research question

How does CivicLens, a public transparency and oversight agent, balance transparency, privacy, procedural fairness, uncertainty, and cooperation when justice-related decisions are made under pressure in a multi-agent legal simulation?

### CivicLens

CivicLens is designed to surface accountability concerns, request verifiable information, distinguish established facts from allegations, and support lawful public oversight. It should encourage transparency without treating publicity as automatically more important than due process, privacy, safety, or the integrity of an investigation.

## Important scope statement

This is a simulated educational study. It is not real legal advice, does not perform real legal work, and is not a production-system evaluation. No simulation output should be treated as a legal conclusion, professional recommendation, evidence about a real person or legal system, or proof of stable behavior outside the documented conditions.
