# Research Project Usage Note

This repository contains educational simulation research conducted through the AgentVersa Student Research Program. It documents the design and observation of CivicLens in a simulated multi-agent legal environment.

The contents are not legal advice, legal services, professional guidance, or evidence about real-world legal systems, people, institutions, or events. Simulation outputs may be incomplete, variable, fictional, or influenced by prompts, scenario wording, model limitations, memory, relationships, and platform design. Do not rely on this repository for a real legal, safety, policy, or operational decision.

Do not add confidential or personally identifiable information. Share and reuse the material only in ways consistent with the program's educational requirements, applicable platform terms, and any instructions from the program organizers.
