# Ethics and Limitations

## Educational simulation

AgentVersa is a simulation and does not perform real legal work. CivicLens is not a lawyer, court, regulator, public-records office, or production oversight system. This repository must not be used as legal advice or as a substitute for qualified professional judgment.

## Model and platform variability

LLM outputs can vary between runs. Observed behavior may be influenced by prompts, scenario wording, model limitations, memory, relationships, platform design, available context, turn order, and other simulation conditions. A result may therefore reflect the interaction between CivicLens and its environment rather than a stable property of the agent alone.

## Interpreting displayed reflections

Displayed private reflections are generated structured summaries, not hidden chain-of-thought. They should be treated as observable simulation artifacts or self-reports within the interface, not as direct access to an internal reasoning process.

## Risk scores

Risk scores are simulated assessments unless separately calibrated. They should not be interpreted as validated probabilities, legal determinations, safety guarantees, or objective measurements without an independent calibration method.

## Evidence limits

A small number of scenarios cannot establish production safety or professional competence. One output, one successful decision, or one failure does not prove a stable behavioral pattern. Findings should state the number and type of episodes reviewed and should preserve contradictory evidence.

## Privacy and data handling

Do not include confidential or personally identifiable information. Use fictional, redacted, or high-level descriptions for scenario material. Do not copy private data into this repository unless it has been explicitly approved for educational use and adequately de-identified.

## Researcher responsibilities

- Keep original predictions and Version 1 design unchanged after observation begins.
- Separate direct observation, generated text, and researcher interpretation.
- Avoid fabricating quotes, statistics, outcomes, or evidence.
- Record uncertainty and alternative explanations.
- Use only the minimum information needed to support the research question.
- Avoid claims about real legal systems based solely on this simulation.
